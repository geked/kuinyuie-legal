Terms of Use

Draft. Our lawyer is reviewing this page. The final version will replace it.

Operator: Kuinyuie Tech, Ghana. Updated 8 November 2026.

1. Who we are

Kuinyuie Ride is an app of Kuinyuie Tech ("we", "us"). It connects riders with independent drivers. We do not own the vehicles and we do not employ the drivers.

By making an account or asking for a ride you agree to these terms.

2. Who can use the app

You must be 18 or older. Riders aged 13 to 17 can use the app only as a teen rider, with a guardian, under the teen rules below.

3. Your account

Give your real name and a working phone number. Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens on your account. You can delete your account in the app at any time (Profile, then Delete my account).

4. Booking a ride

You choose a ride type (Moto, Economy, Comfort, Premium or Courier) and see the fare before you book. Premium cars are no more than 5 years old. A driver may accept or decline, and we cannot promise a driver will be free.

You can book for someone else. You pay, and the driver sees that person's name and phone only after accepting. You confirm they know about the trip.

You can add up to 3 stops. Time at a stop beyond 2 free minutes is charged at the waiting rate.

5. Fares and payment

The fare is shown before you book. It depends on distance, time and the ride type, and it can be higher when many people want rides at the same time. The price you see is the price you pay.

Cash is the way to pay for now. Mobile money and card payments will be added later, through Paystack.

Waiting at pickup is free for the first 2 minutes. After that a small per-minute fee applies, up to a limit.

Promo codes and ride credit lower your fare. Credit can only be used for rides and is never paid out as money.

6. Wait & Save

When many riders are asking for Economy rides and few drivers are free, the Economy ride may show a "Save by waiting" offer. If you take it, you get 10% off and agree to wait up to 15 minutes for a driver. It is only offered for Economy, and only then.

7. Cancelling and refunds

Cancelling before a driver accepts is free. If you cancel after a driver accepts, a cancellation fee applies. If something went wrong with a fare, report it in the app. We review it and may correct the fare or refund you.

8. Your behaviour

Do not harass or threaten drivers, damage vehicles, carry illegal goods, or carry more people than there are seats. Report lost items through the app.

9. Safety tools

SOS and crash alerts text your emergency contacts your location. They help you reach your own people quickly, but they are not emergency services and they do not alert us automatically. In an emergency, call 112 first.

Crash detection uses your phone's motion sensors during a trip. It can give false alarms and can miss some crashes.

10. Teen riders

A guardian must agree to these terms and the Privacy Policy before a teen can book. Teen rides go only to drivers with a high rating and enough finished trips. The teen gets a PIN that the driver must enter before the trip starts. The guardian gets a link to follow the trip live, and the link stops working when the trip ends. Drivers must not contact a teen outside the app or ask personal questions.

11. Ratings, complaints and suspension

You can rate drivers and send complaints. We review them. We may suspend or close an account for safety, fraud, misuse or breaking these terms. A suspended person sees a notice in the app.

12. Our responsibility

We provide software that connects you to independent drivers. The limits of our responsibility, including for injury, accidents and insurance, will be set out here after our lawyer has reviewed this page.

13. Changes and law

We may update these terms and will tell you in the app. Using the app after a change means you accept it. The laws of Ghana apply.
