Privacy Policy

Draft. Our lawyer is reviewing this page. The final version will replace it.

Operator: Kuinyuie Tech, Ghana. Updated 8 November 2026.

1. The short version

Kuinyuie Tech collects only what Kuinyuie Ride needs to run safe rides. We do not sell your personal data.

Kuinyuie Ride and the Kuinyuie driving app use the same account system and database, both run by Kuinyuie Tech.

2. What we collect and why

Name, email and phone number: for your account and so riders and drivers can reach each other on a ride.

Your location: to find drivers near you, show the route, track the trip, and for SOS and crash alerts. Drivers share their location while online.

Trip history (pickup, drop-off, fare, time): for receipts, recent places, disputes and safety reviews.

Driver documents and photos (licence, vehicle, selfie), plate, vehicle model and year, and the mobile money number for payouts: to approve drivers and keep them compliant.

Ratings, complaints and lost-item reports: for quality and safety.

Crash reports from drivers (okay or needs help, a short note, location): so we can follow up.

Fuel and charging prices drivers add (station, price, the station's location, and a random code that stays on the phone): to show prices to other drivers and to stop abuse.

Referral codes and ride credit: to give rewards.

A push notification token for your phone: to send ride and safety notifications.

Motion sensor data: used on your phone to detect a crash. It stays on the phone unless an alert is sent.

Your emergency contacts, language and appearance choices: kept on your phone.

3. Who can see it

We can see account, trip, document and report data to run the service. On a ride, the rider and driver see each other's name, phone and live position. A guardian sees a teen's trip only through the link, until the trip ends. Drivers see a rider's rating but not who rated them.

4. Services we use

Supabase (database and sign-in), Google Firebase (push notifications), Google (route directions), OpenStreetMap and its routing and place-search services, including Photon and OSRM (maps, routes and addresses), Open-Meteo (weather), Netlify (the page a guardian opens to follow a trip) and Paystack (payments, when they start). These services get only what they need, such as a location to draw a map or find a route.

Kuinyuie Ride shows no advertisements and does not use your data to target ads.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account we remove your profile and keep only what the law or an open complaint requires. Exact periods will be set after our lawyer has reviewed this page.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and you can stop location sharing at any time in your phone settings. Write to kuinyuie.app@gmail.com.

7. Children

Teen riders aged 13 to 17 need a guardian's agreement. Teen data is used only for the ride, safety and the guardian link. It is never used for advertising.

8. Security

Data is kept on access-controlled servers and sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly safe. If there is a serious breach we will tell the people affected and the regulator, as the law requires.

9. Changes

We will post updates in the app and show the date of the latest version.
