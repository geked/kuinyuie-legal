Kuinyuie Ride

Driver terms

Draft. Our lawyer is reviewing this page. The final version will replace it.

Operator: Kuinyuie Tech, Ghana. Updated 8 November 2026.

1. Independent driver

You run your own business. You choose when to go online. You are not our employee and we do not promise any income.

2. Who can drive

You need a valid driver's licence for your vehicle, the right to use the vehicle, and to pass our review, including a selfie check. We may refuse any application.

3. Documents

Keep your licence, vehicle registration, roadworthiness certificate and insurance valid. The app records their expiry dates, warns you before they end, and stops you taking rides once one has expired.

4. Vehicle and ride type

You apply as Moto, Economy, Comfort, Premium or Courier. Premium needs a car no more than 5 years old. You can also take lower ride types if you switch that on in your menu.

5. Fees and earnings

We keep a booking fee and a commission on each ride. You receive the rest. Promo, credit and Wait & Save discounts are paid from our share, not yours. Waiting fees are paid to you. Your Earnings screen shows your share and what you owe us for cash trips.

6. Payouts

Earnings are paid to a mobile money number you give us, through Paystack. The number locks after you save it. To change it, contact us.

7. Your conduct

No driving under the influence, no harassment, no discrimination, no trips outside the app, and no sharing your account. Obey traffic law and speed limits. Do not use your phone by hand while driving.

Teen rides: follow the PIN rule, never contact a teen outside the app, never ask personal questions or take photos.

8. Fuel and charging prices

The Fuel & charging screen shows prices other drivers reported. They may be out of date or wrong, so check at the pump. Only add a price you really saw. False prices can lead to a ban. Look at the screen only when you are stopped, and not during a trip.

9. After a crash

If the app detects a likely crash during a trip, it shows an "Are you okay?" screen. You can tell us you are okay or that you need help. If you need help, we get your message, your note and your location. We do not promise a response time, so call 112 first if someone is hurt. Report every accident or incident through the app and cooperate with the police.

10. Ratings, complaints and ending

Low ratings, serious complaints or expired documents can lead to suspension or removal. You can reply to a complaint. Either of us can end this agreement at any time, and we can end it at once for safety reasons.

11. Your responsibility

You are responsible for your vehicle, your insurance and your fines. The exact limits of our responsibility will be set out here after our lawyer has reviewed this page. The laws of Ghana apply.
