Kuinyuie

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 26, 2026

Kuinyuie helps drivers around the world see speed cameras, speed bumps, police and other road hazards where map data is available, lets drivers share what they see, and can alert your emergency contacts after a detected crash. This policy describes which information the app handles, what it is used for, and what you can do about it.

Operator: Godfred Kumahor, Ghana
Privacy contact: kuinyuie.app@gmail.com

1. The short version

  • Location is the core of the app. It is used while the app is open to warn you about hazards ahead.
  • Reports you send (police, speed bump, hazard and similar) are shared with other drivers, without your name.
  • Emergency contacts are stored on your phone. If crash detection is on and a crash is detected, the app texts them from your phone.
  • You can use the app without an account. An account is needed only for shared features such as family fleets, the leaderboard and points.
  • You can delete your account and your data from inside the app.
  • Free accounts see ads from Google AdMob. Premium accounts see no ads.
  • The app does not use analytics or third-party crash-reporting tools.
  • Our servers and service providers may be in countries other than yours. See section 4.

2. Information the app handles

Location and driving data
- Your GPS position, speed, direction and time, while the app is open. Used to show your position and warn you about hazards ahead.
- Trip history, if you record trips. Trips stay on your phone unless you share them.
- Live location, if you choose to share it with family members or someone you follow by code.

Crash detection and emergency contacts
- If you turn on crash detection, the app uses your phone's motion sensors to look for signs of a crash.
- If a crash is detected, the app asks you whether you are okay. If you do not respond, it sends a text message to the emergency contacts you saved, with your name, the time and, where available, your location.
- You can choose to add medical information, such as blood group or allergies. If you do, it is included in the crash text sent to your emergency contacts.
- Emergency contact names and phone numbers, and any medical information, are stored only on your phone, in encrypted storage.

Reports and votes
- The type of report (for example police, speed bump, traffic jam, accident, road works or a fixed point), its location and time, any description you add, and whether other drivers confirm or remove it.
- Corrections you send for road speed limits, hazards or fixes, and your votes on those.
- Some countries restrict certain alert types, such as speed camera and police warnings. In those countries the app hides these alerts and does not let you report them, so no such reports are made from there.
- Reports are stored with an anonymous identifier, not your name. Reports you send after deleting your account stay in the app without a name.

Fuel prices
- If you submit fuel prices, the price, the station or place, and the time.

Account details (optional)
- Name and email address, plus a password stored by our authentication service. We never see or store your password in readable form.
- Points and leaderboard standing, if you join those features.
- Family fleet membership, and any location or trip sharing you choose to turn on.

Voice commands (microphone icon)
- When you tap the microphone icon and speak, the app turns your speech into text using your phone's speech recognition service. It looks only for hazard words in that text, such as "speed bump", "police" or "accident", and uses them to place that hazard on the map at your current location.
- The app does not save the text of your voice commands. The words are only used to find a hazard type, and the report sent to us contains the hazard type and your location, not the words you said.
- Speech recognition is done by your phone's speech service. The app does not request on-device recognition, so on most Android phones, which use Google's speech service, your audio may be sent to Google and handled under Google's privacy policy.

Camera and dashcam
- If you use the dashcam feature, recordings are saved to your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share it.

Phone, SMS and motion
- The app sends text messages when a feature asks it to, such as emergency alerts and test messages. It does not read your messages or call history.
- The app uses your phone's activity and motion sensors to detect driving and to help with heading and speed.

Device and app information
- App version, device model, operating system version, and the data needed to keep the app running. The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Purchases (when Premium opens)
- Premium payments are taken through Google Play billing. We receive purchase status and plan information, not your card or mobile money details.

Advertising (free accounts only)
- Free accounts see an ad when the app opens, and only while the car is stopped. Google AdMob selects and shows the ad.
- AdMob may receive your advertising ID, device and app information, and approximate location or IP address derived from your connection, so it can show relevant ads. Where required, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. If you refuse, you still see ads, but non-personalised ones, and the rest of the app keeps working as normal. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
- Premium accounts see no ads.

Map, search and road data
- Map tiles come from CARTO and OpenStreetMap. When this build has a Google Maps key, Google Maps is used instead.
- Searching for places uses Photon (Komoot). Route planning uses OSRM and Valhalla. Road and camera data is loaded from OpenStreetMap through Overpass API servers.
- These requests can reveal your approximate location to those providers, along with the area you are looking at.

3. How we use it

  • To show your position and warn you about cameras, bumps, police and other hazards.
  • To send emergency alerts to your contacts when you ask for them or when a crash is detected and you do not respond.
  • To save, confirm, remove and share reports so other drivers benefit.
  • To run accounts, family fleets, live following, points, the leaderboard and Premium features.
  • To block abuse, remove false reports and keep the map accurate.
  • To send you messages from the app team, such as safety notices or important changes.

We do not sell your information. We do not use your GPS location to choose ads. Google AdMob receives the advertising data described in section 2.

4. Where data is stored and who sees it

  • Where it is stored. Our database is hosted by Supabase, and our service providers may process data in other countries. We choose providers that protect data under appropriate contracts and safeguards.
  • Other drivers see anonymous reports and confirmations you make, without your name.
  • Family members and people you follow see the live location and trips you agree to share, and only while sharing is on.
  • Your emergency contacts receive the alert text when it is sent, which includes your name, the time and any location the app includes.
  • Service providers that help run the app: our database and hosting provider, Supabase; the map, search, routing and road data providers listed above; Google AdMob, for ads shown to free accounts; and payment processors once Premium is live. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • App administrators can see reports, blocked accounts and support requests to keep the service safe.
  • Authorities may receive information where applicable law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Reports: until they expire or are removed. Police reports expire after a short period unless someone confirms them. Confirmed reports may stay longer.
  • Trips, dashcam recordings and emergency contacts: on your phone until you delete them.
  • Live location: only while sharing is on.
  • Server logs: 1 day.

When you tap Delete my account in the app, your sign-in record is deleted from our database straight away. Your account details, points and family fleet membership are removed automatically along with it, and no manual step is needed. Reports and corrections you sent stay on the map, but they are no longer linked to your name.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off location, microphone, camera, SMS, phone and activity permissions in your phone's settings. Features that depend on them will stop working.
  • Turn off crash detection or remove emergency contacts in the app at any time.
  • Stop live sharing in the app at any time.
  • Delete your trips, dashcam files or reports you sent, in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app: Account, then Delete my account. You can also ask for this at kuinyuie.app@gmail.com.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to have it deleted or restricted by writing to kuinyuie.app@gmail.com. We will reply within 10 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your local privacy laws, and we will respond to those as well.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Your sign-in details, emergency contacts, medical information and trip history are kept in encrypted storage on your phone. Access to the database is restricted to the people and systems that need it. Admin tools are limited to accounts with that role. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your phone and password safe.

8. Children

The app is for adults. Accounts are for users aged 18 and over. If you believe someone younger has given us information, contact kuinyuie.app@gmail.com and we will remove it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before the change applies.
