1. What the app is

Kuinyuie tells you about speed cameras, speed bumps, police and other road hazards near you, shows your speed and the posted limit where it is known, and lets you and other drivers report what you see. It can also detect possible crashes and text your emergency contacts, and it can share your live location with people you choose. The information comes partly from OpenStreetMap and partly from reports by drivers. Coverage varies by country and area. Information can be wrong, incomplete or out of date.

2. Driving comes first

  • The app assists you. It does not replace your attention, your judgement or the traffic law where you are driving.
  • Do not touch or look at the screen while driving. Use voice features where possible, and never use the report buttons while the car is moving.
  • You are responsible for your speed and your driving. A missing or late warning is never a reason to drive faster.
  • In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 works in many countries). Do not rely only on the app.
  • Some countries restrict certain alerts. Currently, warnings about speed cameras and speed checks are switched off in Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Germany, France, Cyprus and North Macedonia, and warnings about police checks are switched off in Switzerland and Liechtenstein. In these countries the app hides these alerts and does not let you report them. We have built these limits in, and you must not try to turn them back on, work around them, or use other apps or settings to get them. We may update this list as the law changes.
