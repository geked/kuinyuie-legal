Kuinyuie

Delete your account

You can delete your Kuinyuie account and its data at any time. You do not need to contact us first.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Kuinyuie.
  2. Open the menu and tap Account.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Delete it by email

If you cannot open the app, write to kuinyuie.app@gmail.com from the email address on the account, asking us to delete it. We will delete the account and reply within 10 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name and email address
  • Your password and sign-in details
  • Your points and leaderboard standing
  • Your family fleet membership and any sharing it allowed
  • Your Premium status

Trips, dashcam recordings, emergency contacts and any medical information are stored only on your own phone. They are removed when you delete the app, or you can clear them in the app at any time.

What is kept

Road reports you sent, such as a speed bump, a police check or a speed limit correction, stay on the map so other drivers are still warned. They are no longer linked to you or your name after your account is deleted.

Server logs are kept for 1 day.

Questions

Write to kuinyuie.app@gmail.com.
